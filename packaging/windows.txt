VIDEO MERGER

1. Double-click VideoMerger.exe. It opens in your web browser.
   (First time only: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC",
   click "More info" then "Run anyway".)
2. Drag your clips into the page.
3. Drag the clips in the timeline to put them in order.
   Click a clip to watch it, or press "Play all".
4. Set up your watermark once in the Watermark section.
   It is saved automatically for next time.
5. Press "Export video". The finished video goes to the "output"
   folder (or the folder you choose).

Close the browser tab when you are done. The program stops by itself.
Keep ffmpeg.exe in the same folder as VideoMerger.exe.
